The Glass Venue is a private event venue in Miami, a fusion of modern glass architecture and water set on five acres of grounds.

Ceremonies for up to 400 guests take place in an outdoor area with a 100 ft waterfall. Guests then move to the climate-controlled Cocktail Building, surrounded by koi ponds, and on to the Reception Hall, with 17-foot ceilings and floor-to-ceiling glass walls. Kosher events are welcome, with preferred kosher caterers on the venue's list.
