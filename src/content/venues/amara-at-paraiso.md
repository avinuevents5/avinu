Amara at Paraiso is a Latin American waterfront restaurant on Biscayne Bay in Miami, from James Beard Award-winning Chef Michael Schwartz, with floor-to-ceiling windows, an outdoor waterfront deck, two full bars and a rooftop lounge with a wrap-around terrace.

Events can be semi-private or fully private: the main floor with its covered patio and waterfront beach, the rooftop and its terraces, or a full-restaurant buyout, with family-style menus built around Latin American flavors and coastal ingredients.
