The Club at Weston Hills is a private country club in Weston, Florida, set on two championship courses by Robert Trent Jones Jr. that have hosted the Honda Classic.

Couples say "I do" on a patio overlooking the fairways, fountains and tropical landscaping, then celebrate in the Royal Palm, an elegant room with floor-to-ceiling windows. The clubhouse seats up to 320 guests, with an in-house kitchen and bar and a dedicated events team.
