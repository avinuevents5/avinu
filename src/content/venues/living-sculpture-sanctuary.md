Living Sculpture Sanctuary is a family-owned botanical sanctuary in Davie, set among century-old oak trees, cascading waterfalls, koi ponds, a bonsai wall and a Zen rock garden.

Ceremonies unfold in the gardens, while receptions move to the Sanctuary Center, a covered open-air pavilion with an onyx bar, chandeliers and a Japanese-style bridge over the koi ponds. Celebrations welcome up to 170 guests, and the sanctuary’s preferred vendors include kosher caterers.
