Formerly known as the Viceroy Miami, the W Miami sits in the Icon Brickell complex, right where the Miami River meets Biscayne Bay, in the heart of the city’s financial district.

The Great Room is a 2,670-square-foot ballroom finished in ivory and chocolate tones, weathered woods, grass cloth and grained leather, seating up to 200 guests or hosting 250 for a reception. For something more open-air, the Tulum Terrace welcomes up to 120 seated or 200 for a reception, ideal for a ceremony or cocktail hour under the city lights.

In-house catering, bar service and event coordinators handle the details, and on-site rooms keep out-of-town guests just an elevator ride away.
