Formerly known as the Viceroy Miami, the W Miami sits in the Icon Brickell complex, right where the Miami River meets Biscayne Bay, in the heart of the city's financial district.

The Great Room is a 2,666-square-foot ballroom finished in ivory and chocolate tones, weathered woods, grass cloth and grained leather, seating up to 180 guests or hosting up to 275 for a standing reception. For something more open-air, the Outdoor Fireplace Terrace welcomes up to 150 seated or 200 standing guests, ideal for a ceremony or cocktail hour under the city lights.

In-house catering, bar service and event coordinators handle the details, and on-site rooms keep out-of-town guests just an elevator ride away.
