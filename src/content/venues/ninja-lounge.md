The Ninja Lounge obstacle and trampoline zone now lives inside Dezerland Action Park Miami, a 250,000-square-foot indoor entertainment complex in North Miami with attractions for every age.

It is a natural fit for kids’ parties, bar and bat mitzvahs, end-of-season celebrations and team-building events. Party packages include food options, and the entire facility can be booked for a private event. No set up, no clean up — just bring your group and an attitude ready for fun.
